# Writing workspace

Scope: the manuscript scene editor, the surface writers and worldbuilders spend evenings in. Mode: Operate. Build path: code-led.

Audience and job: one person drafting and planning in the same sitting; they write prose, and the world's bookkeeping (who speaks, what holds, what is revealed, when it is) gathers around the text without being asked for.

Constraints: browser-only; per-speaker tints are assigned per scene, only to speakers present, always paired with the margin cue; Subtext is opt-in per scene until its inference proves accurate; default layer state is Speakers only.

Unresolved: a two-pane split (scene beside an entry) on top of tabs; how the subtext layer is produced (bookkeeping pass, increment worldcrafter-8agg); Fountain-style notes, synopses and boneyard in the content schema (worldcrafter-3lho).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The manuscript is the page and the world is its apparatus: layers lie behind the prose and markup sits around it, attached to the words it is about. Refuses the binder-editor-inspector triptych and any chat panel.

OWN-WORLD: Night Edition. Warm near-black ground #14120f, bone ink #e6dfcf in three steps, hairline rules #2f2a23. Old Standard TT for prose and display, Source Sans 3 for apparatus. One vermilion #e34b2f, reserved for AI-made text: the double-ruled plate. Layer hues each do one job: speaker tints, time blue #8da2f0, subtext gold #e8b923. Gloss notes are lettered keys on leader lines, never cards.

STORY: The writer sees the scene, knows who is speaking and when it is, trusts that everything inferred is marked as inferred, and keeps writing; they confirm or dismiss a note in one click, without leaving the text.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left rail 300px: Manuscript/World/Topics, scenes in reading order with the chronology spark beside them, speakers in this scene, world inbox at the foot. Header 56px: a tab strip of open documents (scenes and entries alike; the active tab carries the crumb), story-time stamp, layer toggles, Context, Generate next (primary, right). Prose column 620px at 19px/1.62 with Fountain caps cues in the left margin; gloss column 340px to its right. Signature interaction: toggling a layer draws its marks and leader-lined notes in place, under 200ms, with no reflow of the prose. Motion grammar: marks fade and underlines draw left to right; nothing slides or bounces.

FORM: Shoulder notes and plate (challenger rw-lexicon-shoulder-and-plate, Night Lexicon), first on the ordered list after fusing with The Gloss's quiet metadata and The Prompt Book's attached cues. seed:20747c079da331ea

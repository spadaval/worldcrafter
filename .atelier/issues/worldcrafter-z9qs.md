## Outcome

A writer can hold a story at any level of detail and change it at the highest level where a change applies. The structure is a graph of actors, drives, questions, events and scenes, seen through outline and weave-grid views. Scenes can be rearranged with their prose travelling with them, prose is anchored to the structure and marked stale when it changes, and the app points out structural problems while they're cheap to fix. The strategy (`docs/strategies/structure-first.md`) owns the intent, the success criteria T1–T6, the shape and the authority.

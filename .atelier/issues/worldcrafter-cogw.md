## Context

"WorldCrafter" gets the point across but feels generic. The name should come from the story model (decision worldcrafter-ok5a): building stories from the frame out, layers under the finished text, threads woven together, the film-editing model. Sumeet deferred this on 2026-10-29 as needing more time.

Considered so far (domain status from RDAP lookups on 2026-10-29, unconfirmed with a registrar):

- **Undertext** (the older writing under a palimpsest): undertext.ink, .dev, .studio looked free. Proposed before the story model; it names layered text, not structure.
- **Rubricate**, **Scholiast**: manuscript annotation. Same problem as Undertext.
- **Armature** (a sculptor's internal frame): armature.ink looked free. Rejected for now: .dev sounds like 3D modelling software, and the word still feels like engineering.
- **Heddle**, **Selvage** (loom parts): .ink looked free. Obscure.
- Taken or crowded: Marginalia, Gloss, Orrery, Palimpsest, Subtext, Quire, Weft, worldcrafter.com. Anything with "loom" is avoided (the base-model writing tool Loom).

Renaming is cheap: the repo and the `worldcrafter-` issue IDs can stay.

## Question

What should the product be called, and on what domain?

Chapter IV · Scene 2

The Sealed Door

The lamps along the Salt Gate had burned down to their wicks by the time Ardelia reached the sealed door. She set her palm against the iron, and it was colder than the sea had been.

“Three hundred years,” said Kesh, behind her. “And it still knows you.” Ardelia did not answer. The door remembered the hand that had sealed it, and that hand had been hers, in the winter Aldric took the crown.
